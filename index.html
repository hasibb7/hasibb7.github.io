Hasib OpenClaw Personal

Hasib OpenClaw Personal is a private, single-user assistant. It runs on its owner's own computer and reads the owner's own Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs and Sheets, in read-only mode, to assemble a daily morning brief and to answer the owner's own questions about that data.

It is not offered to the public. It has one user: the person who built it. It cannot send email, and it does not create, change or delete anything in the owner's Google account.
