Privacy policy

Hasib OpenClaw Personal. Last updated 5 October 2026.

Who this applies to

This application has a single user: its owner and developer. It is not offered to anyone else, and no other person's Google account is connected to it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the application requests read-only access to the owner's Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets. It cannot send email and does not create, modify or delete anything in the Google account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to assemble the owner's own daily brief and to answer the owner's own requests. To do that, relevant content may be processed by the language-model providers the owner has configured for this assistant, currently Google (Gemini) and OpenAI, solely to produce a response for the owner.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than the model providers named above, for the purpose above.

Storage

The application runs on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored in that computer's system keychain. Google data is not copied into any separate database; any local logs remain on that computer.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the developer's GitHub profile: github.com/hasibb7.
